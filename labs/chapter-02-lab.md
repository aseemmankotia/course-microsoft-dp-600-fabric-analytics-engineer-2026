# Chapter 2 Lab: Securing Data: RLS, CLS, OLS, and Dynamic Data Masking

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Walkthrough: define an RLS role with a DAX filter, apply CLS with T-SQL DENY on a column, and apply dynamic data masking to a customer email column in a warehouse

## Key takeaway

RLS, CLS, OLS, and dynamic data masking are applied at different layers and none of them substitute for the others.

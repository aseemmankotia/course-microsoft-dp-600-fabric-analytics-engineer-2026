## Chapter 12: Full Exam Simulation & 100-Minute Pacing Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | DP-600 total exam time and approximate item count? | 100 minutes total; ~59 items (48 standalone + 11 case-study items). Exam tip: budget ~1 min/standalone item, leaving ~52 min for the case-study set plus review buffer. |
| 2 | Recommended per-standalone-item time budget? | ~1 minute each. If you exceed ~90 seconds, flag it and move on — protect your case-study time buffer. |
| 3 | Case-study reading strategy? | Read the scenario/exhibits ONCE fully, then answer all related questions — only jump back to the specific exhibit a question references, never re-read from scratch. |
| 4 | DP-600 domain weights (2026 skills-measured)? | Maintain a data analytics solution: 25-30%. Prepare and serve data: 45-50%. Implement and manage semantic models: 25-30%. Allocate final review time proportional to these weights. |
| 5 | Multi-answer question wording clue on this exam? | This exam is single-answer only. Wording like 'combination' bundles multiple steps into ONE option — it is never a Select-TWO/THREE item. |
| 6 | Why flag instead of guessing and moving on silently? | Flagging locks in a best guess now (never blank) and marks the item for a targeted return pass before submission. |
| 7 | Elimination strategy for a 1-minute scenario question? | Cut options that use deprecated services or violate a stated hard constraint first, narrowing 4 choices to 2 before making the final BEST-fit pick. |
| 8 | Final review time-allocation rule when time is short? | Spend review time proportional to domain weight — most time on flagged items in 'Prepare and serve data' (45-50%), since fixes there have the largest score impact. |
| 9 | Should you fully re-read a case-study scenario for every question tied to it? | No — read once, then reference only the exhibit/tab called out by each specific question to avoid wasting 3-5 minutes per question. |
| 10 | Recommended time buffer before final submission? | Reserve roughly the last 5-10 minutes purely for reviewing flagged items — never begin new unread questions during this buffer. |
| 11 | Trap in 'which combination is the BEST solution' wording? | The correct answer is a single option bundling multiple actions (e.g., 'Enable X AND configure Y') — it is not a select-multiple prompt despite the word 'combination'. |
| 12 | Domain-weight-based study prioritization for final review pass? | Allocate review effort roughly matching domain weight: ~45-50% of remaining time on 'Prepare and serve data' flags, ~25-30% each on the other two domains. |
